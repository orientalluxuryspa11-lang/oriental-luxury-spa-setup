### Step 1: Access Google Business

## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Inconsistent information** → Use same name, address, phone everywhere
❌ **Poor quality photos** → Always use professional, high-resolution images
❌ **Incomplete profile** → Fill all available fields
❌ **Keyword stuffing** → Write naturally, avoid repetition
❌ **Ignoring reviews** → Respond to all feedback within 24 hours
❌ **No updates** → Post content 2-4 times monthly
❌ **Wrong category** → Select most relevant primary category
❌ **Broken booking link** → Test booking flow regularly
❌ **Old information** → Update hours, services, and photos regularly
❌ **No call-to-action** → Always invite customers to book/call
